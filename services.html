Digital Marketing Services FAQs

What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing is the process of advertising your brand via online channels like social media, email, and Google so as to increase revenue.

What is a digital marketing agency?

A digital marketing agency is a team of people who perform your online marketing efforts, such as social media marketing and email marketing, on behalf of your business. It is also referred to as Internet marketing agency. LYFE Marketing is the #1 digital marketing agency for small businesses due to our 15+ year track record of driving revenue.

Why is digital marketing important?

Digital marketing is important because when done well, it helps businesses drastically increase their brand awareness and ROI for a low cost. LYFE Marketing knows what it takes to help businesses from any industry exponentially scale their revenue.

What are digital marketing services?

Digital marketing services (also called online marketing services) include efforts like social media marketing, email marketing and PPC marketing with the aim of growing your business.

How much does digital marketing cost?

Digital marketing services can cost anywhere from $500-$10,000 a month or more depending on how much you’re spending on ads and how much you’re paying an internet marketing agency to manage your digital marketing for you.

What can Internet marketing services help with?

Internet marketing services help businesses get found by the right people so that they scale their revenue, customers and ROI.

What exactly do digital marketing do?

A digital marketer executes internet marketing services such as social media marketing, email marketing, PPC marketing and more on your behalf so as to grow your business.

Would my business benefit from digital marketing services?

Yes, your business would benefit from digital marketing services because there are billions of people on Facebook alone, let alone the other major marketing channels. This means your target customer is online, and an internet marketing company can help you locate them and convert them into a customer.

How do I choose a digital marketing agency?

Choose a digital marketing agency based on these 3 criteria: their pricing, their reviews / testimonials / case studies, and their management style. The cost of the agency needs to be within your budget, they need to have a large portfolio of work to back up their claims, and their communication and partnership with you needs to be one that’s cohesive with your working style.

How cost-effective is digital marketing compared with traditional marketing?

Digital marketing is extremely cost-effective compared to traditional marketing because you can find and advertise to your exact audience for a much lower price. For example, advertising on a billboard can cost upwards of $14,000 a month (without much to any control over who sees the billboard) whereas you can spend $5 a day to run a Facebook ad to a very specific audience.

How do you identify a successful digital marketing company?

The best way to identify a successful digital marketing company is to look at their reviews, case studies and testimonials. A Marketing company can say anything on their website, but you want to ensure they have the proof and results to back up their claims.

What are services in digital marketing?

The most common services in digital marketing are social media marketing, email marketing, PPC marketing, short video marketing, SEO ( search engine optimization ), affiliate marketing and influencer marketing.

What are the 7 types of digital marketing services?

The 7 types of digital marketing services are social media marketing, email marketing, PPC marketing, short video marketing, SEO (search engine optimization), affiliate marketing and influencer marketing.

What are the 4 major types of digital marketing?

The 4 major types of digital marketing are social media marketing, search engine marketing, email marketing and affiliate marketing.

What are the top digital marketing services?

The top digital marketing services are social media marketing, search engine marketing and email marketing.
