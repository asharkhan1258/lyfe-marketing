Short Form Video Marketing Services FAQs

What is short video management?

Short video management is when a short video management agency like LYFE Marketing performs customer research and competitor research, to create short videos for your brand like Reels & TikToks that attract new followers and customers.

What is short form video marketing?

Short form video marketing is when you publish short videos like Reels and TikToks from your brand account in order to generate more views, followers and brand awareness.

What’s considered a short form video?

Short form videos are vertical videos that are typically 10 minutes or less.

Why is short video marketing effective?

Short video marketing is effective because today’s consumers need trust a brand in order to buy from them, and one of the quickest ways to build trust is with authentic, value-packed short videos.

Where can I post short form content?

You can post short form content on most social media platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Pinterest, and you can also host short form content on your website.
