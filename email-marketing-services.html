Our email marketing services will ensure that every campaign you send is personalized and catered to a particular audience’s needs. Boost Brand Awareness

Every email you send to your subscribers will remind potential customers of your business and your brand. And by consistently sending valuable email campaigns, you will start to create a connection with them.

We can help you create strategic, engaging, and valuable email campaigns to turn more leads into clients and eventually, into loyal patrons of your brand.

Easy To Measure

One of the great things about digital marketing is the fact that all campaigns and strategies you implement can be measured. Email marketing included. It helps to have specific goals that you want to achieve for a certain campaign that you can measure.

With our email marketing services, we use only the best email marketing tools. This helps us report high-quality metrics back to you so you know which campaigns are being effective and which ones need optimization. We will then use this data to improve your email campaigns and get better results.

How Do We Help?

There’s a lot that goes into building successful email marketing campaigns. Thankfully, with the help of our pros at LYFE Marketing, you don’t have to navigate this process on your own. Make Sure Your Emails Are Reaching Your Audience

Businesses often get caught up in email open rates to measure the effectiveness of their campaigns. However, an email can’t be opened if it never reaches the intended person. No fancy email template can solve this issue. With the help of top-rated email marketing professionals like the ones at LYFE Marketing, we will help your business increase its deliverability rates.

Methods Of Building Your Email List

There are many ways you can help build your email lists. One way is through signup forms on your website. WordPress has many methods of implementing these forms into your overall web design. Another method is through social media. Many users sign up with a Gmail account. You can also use social media to run advertisements to collect this information if it isn’t readily available to you. For example, you can run a Facebook ad for a 25% off coupon where all the consumer has to do is input their email address and the coupon will be sent directly to them.

Make Sure Your CRM Is Helping Your Cause

If your preferred CRM and email marketing software don’t communicate with one another, although it may not be the best from a functionality standpoint, it’s not the end of the world. Certain CRM platforms can provide your business with valuable information pertaining to your sales funnel. Most CRM platforms, like Salesforce, will track both your email support and phone support options. This is important when a lead unsubscribes from receiving your business’s emails. If a lead opts out of receiving emails, your salespeople will want to make note that SMS will be the only method of contact they have with that lead moving forward. Unless that same lead decides to opt-in for receiving emails again, of course.

Email Automation

Email marketing automation will make your life easier. Email automation is exactly what it sounds like. Emails will be sent out automatically on behalf of your business. One time this may be useful is for all new customers that provide their email at checkout. You can set up an automated welcome email that welcomes them to your business and encourages them to become loyal customers. Another great option is the use of email automation for transactional emails. The options are endless for what features of your email marketing campaigns you would like to be automated. With the help of our email marketing professionals, you won’t have to figure it out on your own.

You can also use automation tools like an autoresponder. An autoresponder is a program that will automatically answer emails sent to it. These can range from very simple to more complex messages. If you receive a high number of emails and it takes your business time to answer all of them, an autoresponder might make sense for customer support purposes. You’ll be able to have an automated “thanks for your feedback” email sent to the customer letting them know that someone from your customer support department will get back to them shortly.

Segmentation

Email segmentation is a necessary part of email marketing campaigns. Without segmentation, your entire audience would receive the same emails. When consumers or leads receive an email, it should be specific to their interests and needs. As part of our email marketing services, we provide list segmentation for your business. List segmentation is taking your entire email audience and dividing them into smaller categories or lists. This method of advanced segmentation will ensure that each email that goes out is going to the right audience making your overall email marketing efforts more effective.

Optimized Landing Pages

If you run a Shopify business and are launching a new product, you’ll definitely want to let your audience know. But letting them know isn’t enough to increase sales. By providing a link that takes your audience to an optimized landing page for your new product, sales should spike! Including links to bring your audience to a landing page on your business’s website is a great way of increasing brand engagement, website traffic, and sales.

Choosing An Email Marketing Service Provider

There are many different features available to users that can make deciding on an email marketing service provider a very tough choice. Features such as drag-and-drop builders as your preferred email builder method are important to keep in mind when making a decision.

In today’s digital world, more and more options are becoming available each day for email marketing. Providers like sendinblue, aweber, mailerlite, and convertkit might be the right choice for your business. It all depends on what you are looking for. Are you looking for the simplicity of a drag-and-drop editor? Maybe you need a platform capable of sending out bulk emails. Or maybe you’re looking for more advanced features like the use of plugins.

Regardless, you want the best email marketing software for your business that provides you with a friendly user interface and options that best suit your business. It’s important to not just choose a provider that offers a free plan or a lite version. Especially since many free plans offer limited services and will require you to upgrade to a paid plan in order to send unlimited emails. Our experienced professionals have worked with many of the options available today so we can help your business decide on an email marketing service provider that has all the right marketing features for your business!
