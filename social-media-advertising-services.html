What is a social media advertising service?

How much does social media advertising cost?

Social media advertising services cost anywhere from $650-$2500 a month or more.

Should I pay for social media advertising?

Yes, you should pay for social media advertising services if you want experts with 15+ years of experience managing your ad campaigns and increasing revenue for you!

What is the best social media platform for advertising?

Facebook is the best social media platform for advertising because its user base is so big that any business can find their target audience to advertise to on the platform. Facebook is the standard platform that comes with any of our social media advertising services.

What are social media promotion services?

Social media promotion services are synonymous with social media advertising services; it is when an agency like LYFE Marketing creates and manages your social media ad campaigns on your behalf.

Results

What type of results can I expect?

You will see your followers, engagement, website traffic and conversions increasing monthly.

How much do social media advertising services cost?

Our social media advertising services range from $650 to $2,500 a month.

How many conversions can I expect?

How much do I need to spend to advertise on social media platforms?

You need to spend a minimum of $150 a month in ad spend, but we recommend our clients to consider the average lifetime value of a customer for their business. Once you understand this, identify the maximum you are willing to spend to acquire one customer.
