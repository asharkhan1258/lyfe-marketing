LYFE Marketing’s Social Media Services Process That Gets Results for Small Businesses

1

Get Assigned An Account Manager Dedicated To Your Growth

With our social media marketing services, you will be assigned a dedicated social media account manager to audit your accounts, develop your strategy and execute it. This person will research everything there is to know about your business, target market and competitors and will be your primary contact.

2

Develop A Social Media Strategy That Is Specific To Your Business

The first step we’ll take with your social media campaign is to conduct a full audit on your existing efforts, competitors and target market behavior. Based on this audit, we’ll then create a competitive analysis, develop a buyer’s persona, and define an effective content strategy. We will also develop advertising strategies and funnels for your social campaigns to show you the roadmap to success.

3

Create Engaging Content Calendars And Posts

Once you agree to the social media strategy we develop, we will start executing it. We will develop social media posts that are unique to your brand and relevant to your audience. For quality assurance, this content will be prepared in a monthly content calendar. You will have an opportunity to review and approve before anything goes live on your social media profile. When you sign up for our social media marketing services, we will also use organic tactics (hashtags, engagement, contests, etc.) to grow your social media channels.

4

Reporting And Transparent Communication

We send easy-to-read monthly reports that outline what we did over the last 30 days, what the results of those ads were and what we’re changing for the upcoming month. In between reports, you have access to our team via weekly or bi-weekly meetings and 24/7 access to your campaign analytics.

5

Growth Optimization And Daily Maintenance

Our social media marketing services include daily management and growth optimization. This means we monitor all social media activity (comments, messages, and reviews), and aim to respond within 24 business hours. It also means we will look at the results of our monthly reports and adjust your strategy accordingly. If one tactic is not working, we will pivot. If another tactic is working really well, we’ll double down on it.

What is social media marketing?

Social media marketing is the use of social media to increase brand awareness, build a targeted following, drive website traffic and ultimately increase your sales.

What is a social media marketing service?

A social media marketing service is when a social media marketing company manages your social media accounts for you with the goal of increasing followers, brand awareness, engagement and sales from your social media content.

What does a social media marketer do?

A social media marketer creates content for your social media accounts, replies to all comments and messages on your behalf, and manages your social media strategy to attract new followers and customers.

What’s the difference between social media marketing & digital marketing?

Digital marketing is a term that encompasses all of the many different digital marketing methods you can perform including social media marketing, short video management, PPC (pay-per-click) ads, email marketing , and more. Social media marketing services is one of the many digital marketing services that can help you grow your business.
